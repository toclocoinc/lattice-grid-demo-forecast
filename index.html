Forecast versus actual

US electricity output, month by month. A forecast made from data up to is set beside what actually happened. Drag the scrubber through the 24-month horizon: the grid, the fan (history, forecast and the widening 95% interval) and the decomposition (trend, season, what is left over) all follow it.

Grid — date, actual, forecast, interval, residual
Fan — actual (solid), forecast (dashed), 95% interval
Decomposition — observed, trend, seasonal, residual
Data: IPG2211N, Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System (US), retrieved from FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. Public domain, citation requested. The forecast is precomputed by tools/forecast.mjs (a seasonal decomposition with a trend extrapolation; not investment or planning advice). Lattice Grid 1.86.3 · README.
